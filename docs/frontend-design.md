# Thinking about the Users
The design of this application is for a specific kind of user(s).

User 1: The Sales Team
The Sales Team will be creating orders for products that need to be produced.

Their tasks will be:
1. Creating new orders
2. Editing existing orders
3. Deleting orders
4. Viewing orders

Orders have a set of products assigned to them. When creating and editing orders, the sales team will be able to add, remove, and modify the products assigned to the order.

User 2: The Production Team
The Production Team will be managing the production of products and consumption of Ingredients. Their work will mostly be directed by the Sales Team via the Order Pipeline. They will be able to view the orders and the products assigned to them.

Their tasks will be:
1. Viewing orders
2. Viewing products assigned to orders
3. Managing production of products
4. Managing consumption of Ingredients


User 3: The Inventory Team
The Inventory Team will be managing the stock of products and Ingredients. They will be able to view the orders and the products assigned to them.

Their tasks will be:
1. Viewing orders
2. Viewing products assigned to orders
3. Managing stock of products
4. Managing stock of Ingredients
5. Updating order status and allocated products
6. Creating Products and assigning Ingredients
7. Updating Products and assigned Ingredients
